# Ibrahim Lens Studio — Design System

## Product
Photographer studio desk for Ibrahim Lens. Operator tool (not marketing). Hubs: Dashboard, Gallery, Website, Bookings, Clients, Settings. Light is default; night appearance available.

## Dashboard job
Dense operator desk:
- **Today** strip: next shoot today (or empty), open requests / unpaid counts, amount due
- **Overview**: Revenue (period) + collection rate / booked / amount due / avg fee + revenue chart; Bookings created, Status mix, Conversion
- **Activity**: Action required list (with amounts on payment-due rows), Upcoming week schedule with status
- **Recent photos** secondary
- Period: 7 days / 30 days / All time
- Everyday product labels (Revenue, Action required, Amount due) — not slang like Needs you / Earned / Radar / Latest frames

## Brand & tokens (Studio light)
- Ground `#f7f7f5`, panel `#ffffff`, ink `#1a1a1a`, muted `#6b6b66`, border `#e4e4df`
- Accent brass `#7a5210`, danger `#b3261e`
- Font: Figtree (sans) for UI and hub titles
- Cards: `rounded-xl` + 1px border + white panel; avoid purple gradients, cream terracotta clichés, glow, emoji
- Rail: white/panel, 14rem open / 3.5rem collapsed; phone bottom hub bar
- Content column: max-width ~72rem, generous but not sparse padding

## UX principles
- One calm composition, not a SaaS marketing dashboard
- Numbers are the hero of Study; Radar is actionable list/calendar
- Tap targets ≥44px; tabular nums for money
- Prefer density that still breathes — photographer desk, not crypto chart wall
