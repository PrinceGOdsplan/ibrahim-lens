## Why

Visitors need a focused public site plus legal pages for contact/booking data collection.

## What Changes

- Home with brand hero, Portfolio featured images, services/testimonials, Book CTA that **scrolls to booking form**
- About, Portfolio, Work (website-visible only), Contact with booking section
- Privacy and Terms pages
- Same booking form on Home (scroll target) and Contact; request any date/time

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `public-home`: Home composition including scroll-to-booking CTA
- `public-about`: About page
- `public-portfolio`: Portfolio gallery with tag filters
- `public-work`: Work list/detail for website-visible projects only
- `public-contact`: Contact + booking section
- `public-legal`: Privacy and Terms pages

### Modified Capabilities
- (none)
