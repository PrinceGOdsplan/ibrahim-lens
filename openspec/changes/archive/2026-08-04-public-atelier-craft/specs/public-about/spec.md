The system SHALL display the photographer’s about content on `/about` in an editorial photo-and-text rhythm consistent with the Home atelier bar (image plane plus short narrative), not as a plain text dump alone when imagery is available from configured content or brand assets. - **THEN** they see the about content managed via the Website hub with atelier editorial presentation
