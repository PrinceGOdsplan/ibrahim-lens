## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Editorial contact and booking presentation
Contact details, inquiry/booking forms, and FAQ on `/contact` SHALL use editorial atelier presentation (open fields, hairline rules, restrained accent) rather than bordered card panels as the primary chrome. Booking behavior and form definitions remain as specified elsewhere; this requirement covers visual presentation only.

#### Scenario: Contact page craft
- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/contact`
- **THEN** contact details and forms appear in atelier editorial layout without Studio tool chrome and without bordered card panels as the default form container
