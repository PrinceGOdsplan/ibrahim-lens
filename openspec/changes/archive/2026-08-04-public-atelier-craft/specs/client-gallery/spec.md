## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Atelier client gallery craft
The tokenized Delivery gallery at `/g/:token` SHALL use the same public asymmetric atelier visual language as the marketing site (deep ink ground, editorial type, restrained accent). Expiry countdown SHALL read as a quiet accent, not Studio dashboard chrome. Gallery browsing SHALL support immersive image open consistent with Portfolio.

#### Scenario: Delivery gallery matches public craft
- **WHEN** a client opens a valid Delivery link
- **THEN** the gallery presents atelier public styling (not Studio light-tool UI) with a clear quiet time-remaining indicator

#### Scenario: Immersive open in delivery
- **WHEN** a client activates a delivery image
- **THEN** they can view it immersively without watermarks and dismiss the view
