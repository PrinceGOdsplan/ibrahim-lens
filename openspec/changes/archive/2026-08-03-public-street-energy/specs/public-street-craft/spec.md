## Purpose

Defines sparse ornamental craft for the public site: film grain, thin ribbon and print accents, and restrained motion that add street/party intensity on the bold dark base without clutter or soft brochure softness.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sparse street-energy ornaments
Public surfaces SHALL include a small set of ornamental accents (film grain and thin ribbon or print marks using the public gold/ink language) that increase artistic intensity without wrapping every section in decoration.

#### Scenario: Home feels intense but not cluttered
- **WHEN** a visitor views the public Home
- **THEN** they see restrained ornamental accents that raise energy while primary hierarchy remains brand, photos, and Book/Portfolio CTAs

### Requirement: Professional restraint
Ornaments SHALL NOT use floating promo badges, neon glow soup, confetti, or dense ribbon frames around cards. Motion SHALL remain limited (about 2–3 intentional effects) and SHALL respect reduced-motion preferences.

#### Scenario: Reduced motion
- **WHEN** the visitor prefers reduced motion
- **THEN** ornamental motion is minimized or disabled while static grain/ribbon accents may remain

### Requirement: Shared across public chrome
Street-craft accents SHALL be reusable on public Home and light participation on public shell (nav/footer area) so the site feels one system, not a one-page theme.

#### Scenario: Footer shares the system
- **WHEN** a visitor scrolls to the public footer
- **THEN** craft language matches Home (bold dark, gold sparingly, no soft cream brochure)
