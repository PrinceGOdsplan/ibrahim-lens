## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Portfolio image gallery
The system SHALL display Portfolio-membered images on `/portfolio` as a premium warm browseable gallery with uneven / mixed-scale rhythm and immersive open (no watermarks), aligned with the warm public craft system.

#### Scenario: Visitor browses portfolio
- **WHEN** a visitor opens `/portfolio`
- **THEN** they see Portfolio images only in a premium warm gallery (not a flat equal card grid)

#### Scenario: Immersive open
- **WHEN** a visitor opens an image from Portfolio
- **THEN** they get an immersive viewer with keyboard/focus affordances and no watermark overlay
