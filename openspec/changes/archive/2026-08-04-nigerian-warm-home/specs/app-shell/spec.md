## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Public site navigation
The system SHALL provide public navigation to Home, About, Portfolio, Work, and Contact on warm light public surfaces. Delivery gallery routes SHALL NOT appear in main navigation. Nav SHALL remain film-thin or light over hero surfaces with a dedicated mobile menu.

#### Scenario: Visitor opens the site
- **WHEN** a visitor loads the public site
- **THEN** they see Ibrahim Lens branding and links to Home, About, Portfolio, Work, and Contact

#### Scenario: Mobile menu
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the mobile menu
- **THEN** they can reach the same primary routes without relying on a cramped inline nav

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bold rich footer chrome
The public shell footer SHALL feel bold and rich: strong brand presentation, primary route links, reachable +234 phone and Instagram when configured via Website globals, optional Events · Portraits · Fashion hints, and secondary legal links (Privacy, Terms).

#### Scenario: Footer as brand moment
- **WHEN** a visitor scrolls to the footer on a public page
- **THEN** the footer reads as a deliberate brand block, not a minimal legal strip alone
